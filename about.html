Company Overview

CIDAK AUTO PARTS LTD

Independent leading aftermarket automotive replacement spare parts distributor across the UK and Europe.

Corporate Heritage

Global Partnership Approach & European Distribution

CIDAK AUTO PARTS LTD is an independent leading company in the United Kingdom and Europe, distributing aftermarket automotive spare parts to wholesalers and distributors worldwide.

Our Head Office is located in Ascot (35 minutes from Central London) with strategic 3PL warehouse facilities in the UK, Slovenia, and Bulgaria.

"Our aim is to offer CIDAK branded aftermarket parts at very competitive prices and premium quality. We aim to build up a strong and long-term partnership with our customers. This partnership approach allows us and our partners to continue growing together and enjoying commercial benefits for the long term."
