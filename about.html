About CIDAK AUTO PARTS LTD

Delivering OE-Quality Automotive Components to International Wholesalers

Established Distribution Network

Ascot Head Office & European Logistics Hubs

CIDAK AUTO PARTS LTD operates out of Ascot, United Kingdom (just 35 minutes from Central London), providing international wholesalers and auto parts distributors with direct access to matching original equipment standard friction, filtration, and chassis parts.

Supported by strategic 3PL logistics facilities in Slovenia and Bulgaria, CIDAK ensures rapid dispatch of containers and pallet loads across European and global markets.
