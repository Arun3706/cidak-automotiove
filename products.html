Safe & Noise-Free Braking

CIDAK Brake Pads

When it comes to "Safe & Noise Free Braking", the CIDAK brake pad range offers an exclusive product to the automotive aftermarket with consistent braking performance.

  • Designed and produced in high standards manufacturing facilities under strict quality control.
  • All CIDAK brake pads are ECE R90 (E-Marked) approved, confirming performance within +/- 15% of original part.
  • Unique friction material compound blends of raw material ("ceramic material" and "semi-metallic") delivering excellent braking results.
  • Equipped with RMR (Rubber Metal Rubber) anti-noise shims to eliminate brake squeal.
  • Completely "Copper Free" to comply with EPA environmental standards.
  • Consistent long-lasting performance proven across vehicle dyno testing.

Excellent Braking & Anti-Corrosion

CIDAK Brake Discs

When it comes to "Excellent Braking", the CIDAK brake disc range offers consistent rotor performance engineered under strict quality controls from foundry casting to CNC machining.

  • Designed and produced in high-standard manufacturing facilities.
  • High quality standards maintained during raw material selection, iron melting, foundry casting, and CNC multi-axis machining.
  • Unique protective "silver coating" prevents corrosion, presents a stunning aesthetic, and allows immediate fitting without needing to de-grease oil/dirt from the disc surface.
  • ECE R90 (E-Marked) approved across all rotors.

5 High-Efficiency Filtration Lines

CIDAK Filters (Air, Oil, Fuel, Cabin & Carbon)

High quality filtration is essential for modern engines requiring superior filter media. CIDAK filters are manufactured close to original part specifications across Air, Oil, Fuel, Cabin, and Activated Bio-Carbon filters.

  • Air Filters: Designed with precise pleat geometry for proper airflow and secure housing fitment.
  • Oil Filters: Vital for feeding dirt-free oil to engines. Covers metal spin-on and ecological element filters, all pressure tested.
  • Fuel Filters: Removes impurities and water from fuel to protect high-pressure injection systems against engine damage.
  • Cabin & Carbon Filters: High-grade paper and activated carbon media delivering fresh, odor-free cabin air.

CIDAK Steering & Suspension

Steering and suspension are safety-critical components. CIDAK manufacturing partners design and produce parts using modern technology, covering Control Arms, Ball Joints, Stabilizer Links, Bushes, Tie Rods, and Suspension Kits.

  • Raw materials selected as per original manufacturer standards maintaining tight tolerances.
  • Modern production technology including multi-axis CNC machinery and robotic welding.
  • Tested at each manufacturing stage for mechanical performance and joint articulation reliability.
  • Protective paint coatings subjected to salt spray testing to prevent underbody corrosion.
