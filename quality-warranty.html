CIDAK Quality Map & Inspection Stages

How product quality is monitored at each stage until delivery to our distributors.

STAGE 1

Raw Material Inspection

Chemical analysis of high-carbon grey iron, copper-free ceramic friction formulas, and high-purity filter media.

STAGE 2

Foundry & CNC Machining

Computer Numeric Controlled (CNC) turning ensuring parallel DTV disc tolerances and ball joint precision.

STAGE 3

ECE R90 Performance Testing

E-Mark approval validating brake pad and rotor friction performance within +/- 15% of original parts.

STAGE 4

Salt Spray & Pressure Testing

240h+ salt spray anti-corrosion paint testing on chassis parts; pressure-leak testing on oil and fuel filters.

STAGE 5

Packaging & RMR Shim Fitment

Noise dampening rubber-metal-rubber shims installed and parts sealed in protective branded boxes.

STAGE 6

3PL Logistics Dispatch

Final warehouse inspection before container dispatch from UK, Slovenia, or Bulgaria logistics centers.
