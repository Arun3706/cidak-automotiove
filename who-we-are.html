Who We Are

Ascot UK HQ & European Distribution Heritage

Engineering Reliability & Global Distribution Partnership

CIDAK AUTO PARTS LTD is a UK-registered company dedicated to supplying premium quality automotive replacement parts directly to international wholesalers, buying groups, and large-scale stockists.

Headquartered in Ascot, United Kingdom (just 35 minutes from Central London), CIDAK combines British commercial governance with a robust European logistics infrastructure comprising 3PL warehousing facilities in Slovenia and Bulgaria.
